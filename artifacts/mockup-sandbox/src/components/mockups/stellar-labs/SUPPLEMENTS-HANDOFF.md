## Merchant-supplied brand callouts

The homepage and supplements preview carry the requested exact callouts: “Easy to Digest,” “Certified gluten free,” “Non-GMO,” “cGMP certified in a USA facility,” “GLP-1 friendly,” and “rGHB free.” The “rGHB” spelling is intentional. These are merchant-supplied, pending product-specific verification; they are not independently verified certification seals, and should not be read as applying to every product. Complete product labels govern. Some labels list gelatin or color ingredients, so the brand-wide callouts do not replace those product-specific facts or warnings.
