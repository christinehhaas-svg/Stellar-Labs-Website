# Shipping & Returns Policy

At Stellar Labs Nutrition, we want you to be satisfied with your purchase. Please review our shipping and return policies below.

## Shipping

Orders are processed during regular business days, excluding weekends and holidays. Shipping costs and delivery options are displayed at checkout.

Once your order ships, you will receive a confirmation email with tracking information, when available. Delivery times may vary depending on your location and shipping carrier.

## Returns & Refunds

Products may be returned for a full refund within 30 days of delivery, provided they are unused, unopened, and in their original packaging.

For health and safety reasons, supplements, protein powders, hydration products, and other consumable products must have their original safety seals intact. Opened or used consumable products cannot be returned.

Books, scales, and other non-consumable products must be unused and in their original condition. Gift cards are non-refundable.

Proof of purchase is required. Once your return is received and inspected, approved refunds will be issued to your original payment method. Original shipping charges are non-refundable unless the return is due to our error.

## Damaged, Defective, or Incorrect Products

If your order arrives damaged, defective, or incorrect, please contact us with your order number and a description of the issue.

Products must be shipped back within 30 days of delivery to qualify for a full refund. Stellar Labs Nutrition will cover return shipping costs for confirmed damaged, defective, or incorrect products.

If a product is leaking, broken, or unsafe to ship, we may request photographs instead of a physical return.

## Product Satisfaction

We want you to enjoy your Stellar Labs Nutrition products. If you're dissatisfied with the taste or experience of a product, please contact us. While opened consumable products are not eligible for returns, we're happy to review your concerns and work toward an appropriate resolution.

## How to Request a Return

Email **allison@washncgroup.com** with your order number and reason for return. We will provide return instructions and the appropriate shipping address.

Customers are responsible for return shipping costs unless the product arrived damaged, defective, or incorrect. We recommend using a trackable shipping service, as we cannot guarantee receipt of packages sent without tracking.
